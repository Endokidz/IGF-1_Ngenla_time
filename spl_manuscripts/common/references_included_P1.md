## Notes on verification

- **Keys whose year differs from the print year.** These keys were kept exactly as supplied, but the citations use the print/issue year. Each print year was confirmed by a PubMed publication-date-filtered search of the PMID.
  - Shah2020: e-pub 2020-05-12; print **2021**;89(3)
  - Badawy2021: e-pub 2021-08-09; print **2022**;58(1)
  - Nanda2023: e-pub 2023-06-30; print **2024**;91(6)
  - Singal2015: e-pub 2015-12-15; print **2016**;48(6)
  - Chikani2014: e-pub 2014-12-01; print **2015**;61(1)
  - Jarrett2013: e-pub 2013-12-23; print **2014**;103(4)
- **Celik2025**: PubMed dates this record 2025-12-13, and a 2026 date filter does **not** match it. The citation therefore uses 2025 with volume 26(1), article 35, exactly as PubMed gives it. The volume number may belong to the 2026 print volume, but PubMed does not show a 2026 date.
- **Alaee2014**: a 2015 date filter does not match it. 2014 is correct.
- **Vasudevan1995**: the second author appears in PubMed only as "Manivarmane", with no initials. It is reproduced as given.
- **Elusiyan**: the initials differ between records. They are JB in Elusiyan2016 and JBE as co-author in Kareem2020, each as given in its own PubMed record.
- **Article numbers instead of page ranges**: Kareem2020 (37:61), AsafoAgyei2017 (2017:2), Soheilipour2018 (9:126), Alaee2014 (e16729) and Celik2025 (26(1):35).
- **Key collisions**: none of these 31 keys appears in `references_verified.md` or `references_included_P3P4.md`.
