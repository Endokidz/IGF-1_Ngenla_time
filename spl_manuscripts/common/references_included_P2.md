## Notes on verification

- **Print year vs key year: no mismatches.** Several records have an e-pub date earlier than the issue date, but in every case both fall in the same calendar year. Examples: Harada2025 (e-pub Aug 2025; 32(11)), Wen2023 (e-pub Jun 2023; 25(6)), Xu2017 (e-pub Nov 2017; 30(12)), Chen2015 (e-pub Apr 2015; 47(6)), Luo2003 (e-pub Mar 2003; 19(1-2)) and Wen2026 (e-pub Jan 2026; 28(2)). Every key year matches the year in the citation.
- **Agarwal2020**: PMID 35187206 was assigned later, but PubMed dates the article 2020-09-23, Glob Pediatr Health volume 7. 2020 is used.
- **No page or article number in PubMed** for MesasArostegui2024 (J Clin Med 13(22)), Wang2025 (Endocr Connect 14(5)), Avril2023 (Endocr Connect 12(4)) and Lambert2025 (Endocr Connect 14(4)). Their citations end at volume(issue) plus DOI. No page or e-locator was added. MesasArostegui2024's PubMed publisher ID is "jcm13226983", but PubMed does not tag it as a page number, so it was not used.
- **Zenaty2006**: the title spelling "occurence" and the co-author spelling "Bouvatier C" are as they appear in PubMed. The co-author does not show in the citation because it lists only six authors before "et al.".
- **Key collisions**: none of these 28 keys appears in `references_verified.md`, `references_included_P3P4.md` or `references_included_P1.md`. Lambert2025 and Lambert2016 are different keys. Ishii2004 and Ishii2014 are different keys.
