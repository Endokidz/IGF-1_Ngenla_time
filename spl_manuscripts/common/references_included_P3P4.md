## Notes on verification

- **Keys whose year differs from the print year.** These keys were kept exactly as supplied, but the citations use the print/issue year. Each print year was confirmed by a PubMed publication-date-filtered search of the PMID.
  - Aslan2013: e-pub 2013-09-05; print **2014**;81(8)
  - Fawaz2020: e-pub 2020-11-06; print **2021**;17(1)
  - Arian2024: e-pub 2024-10-25; print **2025**;21(1)
  - Habous2017: e-pub 2017-11-27; print **2018**;30(1)
- **Methods references with an e-pub year earlier than the print year.** The keys match the print year: Boon2021 (e-pub 2020-10-05; print 2021;12(1)) and Thomson2013 (e-pub 2012-10-12; print 2013;4(1)). Ishii2014 is 2014 in both dates; a 2015 date filter does not match it.
- **Zhang2015**: PubMed lists no DOI field for this record. Its publisher identifier (PII) is `10.7499/j.issn.1008-8830.2015.01.016`. That string looks like a DOI, but PubMed does not tag it as one, so it was not entered in the DOI column.
- **Joya2026**: the fourth author appears in PubMed as last name "Ahmed G Al-Romaihi", fore name "Sheyma". It is reproduced as given.
- **Mogri2013 (item 4)**: Mogri M, Dhindsa S, Quattrin T, Ghanim H, Dandona P. *Testosterone concentrations in young pubertal and post-pubertal obese males.* Clin Endocrinol (Oxf) 2013. It compares 25 obese and 25 lean males aged 14–20 years at Tanner stage ≥4. Testosterone was measured by LC-MS/MS and equilibrium dialysis. Total and free testosterone were 40–50% lower in the obese group. Dhindsa is a co-author.
- **Key collisions**: none of these keys appears in `references_verified.md`. Lee2024 is distinct from Lee2006 there.
