## Shah2020 PMID 32396926 PMC7657971 DOI 10.1038/s41390-020-0950-2 (Pediatr Res) USA, Philadelphia (IFED cohort)
- 388 newborns included (197 males), term 37-42 wk, BW 2500-4500 g, measured within 72 h ("All mother/infant pairs were enrolled and completed the study visit within 72 hours of infant birth.")
- Technique: "One end of a tongue depressor with a measuring strip (with 0.01 centimeter gradation) attached was placed on the pubic symphysis above and immediately adjacent to the penis while firmly applying pressure against the bone. The tongue depressor and penis were held at a 90-degree angle away from the infant’s body. The penis was held and gently stretched while, at the same time, lowering the foreskin until the urethral meatus was visible. The distance from the lower end of the tongue depressor to the tip of the penis was measured to the nearest 0.1 cm."
- "SPL was shorter in White newborns compared to African American (35±5 vs. 36±5 mm, p= 0.04)."
- Overall male SPL mean/SD and per-race male n are in Table 2 which was NOT rendered in retrieved text.
- Same IFED cohort as Chin 2021 (PMID 34013335) -> duplicate cohort.
