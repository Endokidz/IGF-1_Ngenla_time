| 1 | **Tomova A et al. 2010.** Growth and development of male external genitalia: a cross-sectional study of 6200 males aged 0 to 19 years. *Arch Pediatr Adolesc Med* 164(12):1152-7. [doi:10.1001/archpediatrics.2010.223](https://doi.org/10.1001/archpediatrics.2010.223) | The largest age reference: 6,200 Bulgarian boys aged 0-19 years in 20 equal age groups (n = 310 each), with mean (SD) and P5/P50/P95. Probably free on SciELO. | The tables by age and by Tanner stage, and the P10-P90 figure |
| 3 | **Soydan H et al. 2012.** Cross-sectional analysis of penile length in males 13 to 15 years old according to pubertal development stages. *J Urol* 188(4):1319-23. Not indexed in PubMed; available from the J Urol archive or a library. | The historic 0-25-year standard that many clinics still use. | 5 | **Gunter W et al. 2026.** Longitudinal genital growth curves in adolescents and Doppler-based varicocele risk stratification. *BJU Int.* [doi:10.1111/bju.70532](https://doi.org/10.1111/bju.70532) | Prospective **longitudinal** SPL in 407 healthy Belgian controls aged 10.5-18 years, by Tanner pubic-hair stage (1,352 assessments). | The SPL-by-stage table or growth-curve figure for controls, and supplementary tables |
