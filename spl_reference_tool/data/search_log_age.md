## 7. Data-quality flags

- Ryu 2017 (Korea) and Guo 2020 (China) report identical case/control SPL (3.7±0.5 vs 4.3±0.8 cm) - possible duplication; Guo flagged.
- Overlapping samples: Wang 2018 and Yi 2017 (same Chongqing survey); Ryu 2017 within Park S 2016 hospital network/period; Lee JH 2012, Park S 2016 (Ulsan group); Park SK 2021, Ergashev 2021, Lee D 2024 (same Yangsan centre and urologist); Kumanov 2007 is the 0-1 y stratum of Tomova 2010.
- Internal inconsistencies: Roy 2019 neonatal median 2.0 (abstract) vs 2.5 (table/text); Jaiswal 2019 abstract attributes 4.1 cm to 1 y whereas text attributes it to birth; Joya 2026 assigns 3.75±0.4 cm to both 6-12 mo and 6 mo-5 y and its pooled 1-12 y mean exceeds the 6-10 y mean; Gabrich infancy value quoted as 4.7 (Bhat) vs 5.1 at 1 y (Jaiswal).
- Clinic/inpatient samples (not community): Korean series (Park S 2016, Lee JH 2012, Ryu 2017, Park SK 2021, Lee D 2024), Li 2025 (surgical inpatients, possibly under anaesthesia), Puri 2017 (acutely ill inpatients), Joya 2026 (circumcision candidates).
- Tables missing from PMC rendering: Bhat 2015 Table 1, Park SK 2021, Ryu 2017, Lee JH 2012, Lee D 2024, Wang 2018 Tables 1-2, Puri 2017 Table 1 - only text values captured.
- Wang 2018 removed Tukey outliers (1.5 IQR) before fitting - narrows SD/centiles.
- Technique heterogeneity: stretch described as 'as much as comfortably possible'/'till comfortably tolerated' (coded unclear) vs 'fully stretched'/'maximal' vs 'gentle painless extension' (Wang 2018, coded gentle); fat-pad depression not stated in most abstract-only studies.
