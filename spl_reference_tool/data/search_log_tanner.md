## Included (see CSV)

1. **Sotos & Tokar 2017**, Int J Pediatr Endocrinol, PMID 28725240, PMC5513322 (USA). Table 2 gives penile length by Tanner genital stage G1-G5 plus adults: mean ± SD and observed range, with an age range for each stage. Length was gently stretched from the pubopenile skin, so the fat pad was not depressed. The study mixes normal subjects with endocrine-clinic patients, and n per stage is not reported (145 measured in total).
2. **Jaiswal et al. 2019**, Indian J Endocrinol Metab, PMID 31016145, PMC6446671 (India, W. Maharashtra). Table 3 gives mean (SD) SPL for genital stages G2-G5 and pubic-hair stages PH2-PH5, with the mean age in each stage. SPL was maximal and the fat pad was pushed to the bone. Because this study starts at birth (0-18 y), only its Tanner rows are extracted; its age rows belong to the infancy agent. n per stage is not given.
3. **Soydan et al. 2012**, J Urol, PMID 22902017 (Turkey, 13-15 y, military high-school candidates, n=1,539). The abstract gives n and mean age for G1 (5), G2 (194), G3 (965) and G4 (375). Genital stage was defined by Prader testicular volume. **The SPL values by stage are only in the full-text table, which was not retrievable.** This is the top priority for manual retrieval.
4. **Ponchietti et al. 2001**, Eur Urol, PMID 11223678 (Italy, 17-19 y, n=3,300). Abstract only: median stretched length 12.5 cm.
5. **Foresta et al. 2012**, J Endocrinol Invest, PMID 22776895 (Italy, 18-19 y, n=2,019). Abstract only: mean 8.9 ± 1.4 cm. The technique is not stated, and the value is probably flaccid or non-stretched. Flagged.

## Data-quality flags carried into the CSV

- **Fat pad not depressed:** Sotos 2017 measured "gently stretched, from the pubopenile skin". Its values will run systematically lower than pubic-bone SPL (Jaiswal 2019).
- **Gentle versus maximal stretch:** Sotos 2017 used a gentle stretch; Jaiswal 2019 stretched "as much as comfortably possible".
- **Technique unclear:** Soydan 2012, Ponchietti 2001 and Foresta 2012 (abstract only). Foresta's value (8.9 cm at 18-19 y) is implausibly low for SPL and is probably flaccid.
- **Genital stage defined by testicular volume, not visual G rating:** Soydan 2012 (and Saleh 2018).
- **n per Tanner stratum missing:** Sotos 2017 and Jaiswal 2019 do not report it. Weight these studies cautiously when pooling.
